# Personalized outfit matching

The fitting studio has separate opt-ins for saved profile details and style history. History includes at most 20 saved products and 10 non-cancelled orders from the signed-in account. Only clothing attributes and up to 40 ordered-item names, colors and sizes are sent; identity, address, phone, payment data and historical wardrobe photos are excluded. The response reports the history counts actually supplied.

The AI may nominate up to three in-stock catalogue replacements. The backend resolves candidate identifiers against the inventory sent to the model and drops invented or duplicate identifiers. A suggestion replaces the appropriate outfit slot; the customer must run a new analysis. The displayed before/after comparison uses real model scores and does not guarantee an increase. Occasion or personalization-setting changes invalidate the old result and comparison.

Adding a look to the bag keeps the customer in the studio with a View bag action, allowing a profile review before checkout. Saved looks persist the resolved sizes, including a garment opened directly from the wardrobe.
